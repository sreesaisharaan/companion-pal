# Product Brief

## Problem

People juggle tasks, reminders, and spending across disconnected tools. A conventional checklist can feel cold; financial trackers can feel punitive.

## Audience

First release: students and early-career adults who want a simple daily organiser and a lightweight monthly spending view. Design for one person, one currency, and manual transaction entry first.

## Jobs to be done

- When I think of something I must do, help me capture it in seconds.
- When something is due, remind me reliably and let me act from the reminder.
- When I spend money, help me see where this month is going without judgment.
- When I follow through, make progress visible through my companion.

## MVP

- Email/social sign-in; profile and timezone.
- Tasks: quick add, due date/time, completion, list/category, recurring reminders.
- Local notifications and an in-app Today view.
- Monthly transactions: amount, category, date, note; category totals and budget indicator.
- One companion, XP, 3 evolution stages, celebratory feedback, streaks that are recoverable.

## Explicitly later

Bank sync, shared lists, complex budgets, desktop/web client, marketplace cosmetics, AI chat, social features, and more than one pet species.

## Success signals

Activation: user creates a task and completes one within 48 hours. Retention: weekly active users at week 4. Habit: completed tasks per active user. Trust: notification opt-in and zero high-severity privacy incidents.
